Our family's craft

Done by hand, with patience

  • CormsFrom our own field. Each season the largest, firmest corms, around 8 g, are selected by hand and replanted untreated.
  • PickingBy hand at dawn, before sunrise.
  • TrimmingEach stigma trimmed by hand, so its three threads stay joined as on the flower.
  • Pure stigmasOnly the red stigma, with no yellow style.
  • DryingThree months, naturally, laid on cloth in a room sheltered from light and damp.
  • Know-howPassed down from one generation to the next. My family grows it; I check, pack and deliver it myself.

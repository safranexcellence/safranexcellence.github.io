The terroir

A mountain village at 1,400 m

  • PlaceIkenioun, Morocco's Atlas mountains
  • GroundAncient volcanic ground, soil naturally rich in minerals
  • ClimateDry mountain climate, cold winters, snow in some years
  • GrowingNo pesticides, no chemicals
Our family's craft

By hand, with patience and passion

  • CormsFrom our own field. Each September, the largest, firmest corms, around 8 g, are selected by hand and replanted, untreated, with nothing added.
  • PickingBy hand at dawn, before sunrise, over three weeks in November.
  • TrimmingEach stigma trimmed by hand, so its three threads stay joined as on the flower.
  • Pure stigmasOnly the red stigma, with no yellow style.
  • DryingDried naturally, laid on cloth in a room sheltered from light and damp, then left to mature. Three months in all: ready in February or March.
  • Know-howPassed down from one generation to the next. My family grows it; I check, pack and deliver it myself.
